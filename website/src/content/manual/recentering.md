Recentering picks a new target without clicking, and makes your current head position the
middle of the screen. Use it to start tracking hands-free, after the target was lost, or
when you've shifted in your seat and can't reach part of the screen.

## How to recenter

Press **Recenter** or <kbd>F12</kbd>. It works while the camera is running, whether a
target is tracked, lost or not yet picked, and while paused.

1. A white circle appears in the middle of the preview, with "Recentering in 5" below it.
   The status reads **Recentering · 5**, and you have the mouse meanwhile.
2. Look straight at the screen and put the tip of your nose, or the point you want to
   track, inside the circle.
3. The countdown runs 5-4-3-2-1. At the end, the point inside the circle becomes the
   target, the camera takes the pointer at once, and the pointer moves to the middle of
   the [main monitor](../using-the-pointer/#several-monitors).

Pressing **Recenter** again during the countdown starts it over.

## Recentering and pause

Resuming from a pause always recenters: **Resume**, the start key and **Recenter** all
start the countdown, so you line up with the centre circle before the camera moves the
pointer again. See [Pausing](../using-the-pointer/#pausing).

## Recentering doesn't happen by itself

The app never recenters on its own; only you start it. This keeps the pointer from moving
unexpectedly.
